# Independent review items before submission

The English manuscript combines the theoretical construction and finite-error certification. It is not a peer-reviewed final result. Translation, typesetting, and finite numerical replay cannot settle the following questions.

## Mathematical review

1. Check the divisor-closed evaluation basis, completeness of frontier generation, future-result workspace reuse, and the uniform batch-count bound.
2. Check the source-never-target precondition in the two-batch reconstruction, restoration of arbitrary entangled dirty workspace, and rounding and storage bounds at the branch threshold.
3. Check complex Householder elimination, the use of a fixed full-space unitary extension, and accounting for every zero-state reflection.
4. Keep the polynomial exponent fixed in the lower-bound window. Do not transfer the restricted NCT encoder lower bound to general state preparation, or allow the exponent to grow with the width.
5. Check that actual gate sequences, exact inverses, source-output intervals, and compilation-error bounds are bound to the same original target.

## Attribution and presentation

The known sparse-state gate-count order, general CNOT depth and deterministic synthesis, W-state framework, standard-monomial properties, Householder identity, Fourier addition, reflection techniques, and interval arithmetic are not individually claimed as new. Determine whether a published theorem already contains the complete result through an explicit substitution or standard reduction.

The comparison permits the baseline to use the same batching method. Differences between upper bounds are not lower bounds preventing improvements to an entire framework. Historical priority, hardware advantage, and Clifford+T optimality remain unconfirmed.
